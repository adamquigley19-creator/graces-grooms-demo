Useful questions

Designed to answer the little things before they become questions.

The copy below is intentionally clear and can be edited to match the final business policy before launch.

How does the deposit model work?

This demo uses a 25% deposit. The customer sees the full service price, pays the deposit at booking and sees the remaining balance for the appointment. The percentage can be changed in the live version.

Can customers pay in full?

Yes. The demo includes both a deposit option and a pay-in-full option so customers can choose the checkout that suits them.

Will the prices be exact?

The figures shown here are example demo prices. Final pricing can be adjusted to reflect breed, size, coat condition, grooming requirements and Grace's actual pricing.

Can this take real payments?

Yes. This prototype is front-end only. For a real launch, the payment step can be connected to a provider such as Stripe or Square and paired with email confirmations and a booking calendar.
